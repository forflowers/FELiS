# FELIS
## Framework for Evaluating Life-supporting Environments in Space

FELIS is an independent research project exploring planetary habitability and the environmental conditions that may allow life to exist beyond Earth.

The project uses Mars and potentially habitable exoplanets as primary case studies, combining scientific literature, NASA datasets, and computational analysis to investigate how habitability can be evaluated across different planetary environments.

---

## Research Question

**What environmental and planetary factors determine whether a world could potentially support life, and how can these factors be used to evaluate and compare planetary habitability?**

---

## Objectives

- Investigate the major environmental factors associated with planetary habitability
- Examine Mars as a case study of past and present habitability
- Analyze potentially habitable exoplanets using NASA datasets
- Explore how planetary, atmospheric, orbital, and stellar properties influence habitability
- Develop a quantitative framework for comparing planetary environments
- Examine the limitations and uncertainties involved in assessing habitability

---

## Data & Resources

FELIS will primarily use publicly available scientific datasets and resources, including:

- NASA Exoplanet Archive
- NASA Astrobiology resources
- NASA planetary mission data
- Peer-reviewed scientific literature

Specific datasets and sources will be documented as the project develops.

---

## Methods

The project will incorporate:

- Literature review
- Data collection and cleaning
- Exploratory data analysis
- Statistical analysis
- Scientific visualization
- Python-based computational analysis
- Development of a quantitative habitability framework
